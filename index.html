Who we are

Science rooted in the motherland.

Merkhet Group — also known as the Kibale Research Protocol (KRP) — is a startup run by conscious young women and men in Uganda. We blend scientific method with everyday technology to solve real community problems, using materials and minerals sourced directly from our own land.

Our work is grounded in African consciousness and Pan-Africanism, with a clear aim: to tackle the problems conventional education systems have struggled to solve. Through research, unity and a shared desire for independence, peace and progress, we study our history and our environment to build knowledge and self-reliance in African communities.
